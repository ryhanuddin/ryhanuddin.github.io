Research

Research themes in secure networking and intelligent defense

Software-Defined Network Security

I study programmable security mechanisms for software-defined environments, including controller anomaly detection, OpenFlow-aware observability, and mitigation against control-plane and routing-based attacks.

Federated Learning for Cyber Defense

My work explores federated learning for distributed intrusion detection, with emphasis on non-IID environments, cross-regional collaboration, and resilient model aggregation for security analytics.

LEO Satellite and Space-IoT Security

I investigate resilient architectures for secure LEO satellite networking, especially in software-defined satellite network settings where security, latency, and adaptability must be jointly addressed.

Delay-Tolerant Networking

I work on DTN-aware detection and mitigation strategies for blackhole and disruption-based attacks using delivery indicators, canary probes, and hybrid reactive and pre-emptive analytics.
