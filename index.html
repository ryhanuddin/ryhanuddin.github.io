About

A concise overview of my research direction, academic background, and ongoing focus areas.

Research Profile

My work focuses on resilient network security architectures for software-defined satellite networks, Space-IoT systems, and delay-tolerant environments. I study how controller visibility, DTN-aware observability, and federated learning can be combined to support scalable intrusion detection and adaptive mitigation.

I am especially interested in practical security problems involving controller anomaly detection, blackhole attack defense, flooding and routing-based attacks, and cross-regional collaborative learning.

Research Interests

  • Software-Defined Networking and OpenFlow-based observability
  • Federated Learning for distributed cyber defense
  • LEO satellite network security and Space-IoT resilience
  • Delay-Tolerant Networking and blackhole defense
  • Controller anomaly detection and mitigation logic
  • Protocol-aware intrusion detection for cyber-physical systems

Academic Timeline

  • Cleveland State University PhD research in secure SDN, FL, and satellite network defense
  • Research on SDSN-aware IDS and controller resilience Focus on closed-loop detection and programmable mitigation
  • DTN-SHIELD and federated blackhole defense Hybrid reactive and pre-emptive detection in DTN settings

Current Focus

My current work centers on a resilient federated learning-based SDN framework for secure LEO satellite networks, with emphasis on controller health analytics, DTN-aware telemetry, and robust defense against routing and control-plane attacks.
