Professional Summary

I am a cybersecurity researcher and systems professional with work spanning software-defined networking, federated learning, delay-tolerant networking, satellite and Space-IoT security, cloud systems, virtualization, and infrastructure support.

My background combines academic research, teaching support, experimental testbed development, technical writing, and practical experience in systems administration and multi-environment troubleshooting.
